How a live round runs

  1. Place your bets – the timer counts down. Tap a chip, then a bet spot.
  2. No more bets – nothing can be changed now.
  3. The dealer {{ game.id === 'roulette' ? 'spins the wheel' : game.id === 'sicbo' || game.id === 'craps' ? 'shakes the dice' : 'deals the cards' }}.
  4. The result is announced and paid. The next round starts a few seconds later.

Play money only – practise the rhythm and the signals here, then take them to your live table.
